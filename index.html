Greggs plc — DCF Valuation

A discounted cash flow valuation of Greggs plc, built from primary financial statements. Includes a five-year free cash flow forecast, a WACC build from CAPM, and a sensitivity analysis — reaching a base-case value of £8.89 per share versus £19.15 in the market. The gap traces to a deliberately conservative capex assumption, reflecting Greggs' current shop-expansion phase.
